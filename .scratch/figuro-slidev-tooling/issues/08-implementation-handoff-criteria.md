## Question

Once the file, authoring, rendering, and distribution contracts are settled, what scenarios and compatibility boundaries must the implementation-ready specification state so an implementer can build and verify the editor/plugin without further product decisions? Define the minimum end-to-end acceptance criteria and the supported-version matrix needed for a clear handoff.
